BECKHAM PHOTO SHOT LIST
Take a small set in natural window light, with a simple background. Hold the phone vertically and leave some breathing room around the subject for text/crops. Hands-only and over-the-shoulder photos work well too.

1. HERO: over his shoulder while he scrubs a truck in Little Wash. Include his finger touching the screen and enough of the actual app to understand the action.
2. PERSONALITY: a candid reaction while feeding a friend or tapping Hum. Capture what happens naturally; don't ask him to perform a result.
3. DETAIL: close-up of his hands and the screen, with the toy clearly visible.
4. TIMER: the real grown-up timer setting, followed by the actual moon-rest screen. These can be screen recordings to keep the text legible.
5. TOGETHER: your hands beside his while choosing a toy, or an ordinary moment of playing together.

Also take 2–3 short steady video clips (about 5–8 seconds each). They let us reuse the same session for the explainer, Reels and a carousel.

CAPTION A — FOUNDER STORY
Beckham has been helping me test Lull. His latest request was more vehicles for the car wash, so we added them. Lull is a quiet digital toybox for ages 2–6: nine toys to explore, no ads, and a play timer controlled by a grown-up. This is what building it looks like at home.

CAPTION B — TIMER
Even toyboxes need a rest. A grown-up chooses the playtime; when time is up, Lull rests behind its moon screen. Along the way, there are trucks to wash, friends to feed and little discoveries to make.

CAPTION C — PLAY
A muddy truck. A hungry friend. A tiny musical discovery. Nine quiet toys for curious hands—with no ads, no tracking and no app accounts.

KEEP THE FIRST TEST SMALL
Use one real photo/clip with two different opening messages. Change one thing at a time so we learn whether parents respond more to the play or the timer. Look at meaningful visits and interest, not just views. These are creative hypotheses, not conversion promises.
