Support

We read every note. Write to support@lull.app and tell us your device and iOS version if something looks wrong.

Where are the settings?

Tap the small person button at the bottom right of the shelf and answer the grown-up check. The grown-up area has sound, haptics, calmer motion, the play timer, the wind-down hour, which toys are on the shelf, purchases, and this support address.

What is free, and what are the plans?

Bubbles, Little Wash, and Drop Dots are free forever, with no ads and no account. Two optional ways open all nine toys, and the new ones that arrive each season:

Lull Family is a yearly subscription with a free week for new subscribers. It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Keep Forever is a single payment with nothing to renew. Both are bought by a grown-up, behind the grown-up check, through Apple.

How does the free week work?

In the grown-up area, choose Start the free week on Lull Family and confirm with Apple. All nine toys open straight away. Nothing is charged until the week ends, and the grown-up area shows the day it ends. To stop before then, cancel in your Apple Account settings (or tap Manage subscription in the grown-up area) at least a day before; the shelf then goes back to the three free toys.

How do I cancel Lull Family?

Open the grown-up area and tap Manage subscription, or go to Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Lull stays open until the end of the period you have paid for.

How do I get my purchase on another device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the grown-up area, and tap Restore purchases.

The purchase buttons are greyed out.

The prices appear once the App Store answers. Check your connection and tap Reload App Store prices. If In-App Purchases are turned off in Screen Time, the grown-up area will say so.

Lull is resting behind a moon.

That is the play timer a grown-up set. A grown-up can wake Lull through the grown-up check, or change the timer in the grown-up area.

Refunds

Purchases are made through Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
