# Meadow: preserve the painted garden before adding a globe

For this build, use continuous felt ground with an invisible boundary inside the covered camera area and a bounded pool of flower trails. A literal globe changes touch-to-world mapping, camera motion, restored garden coordinates and the relationship between walking and painting. Implementing curvature during a bug-fix pass would put the child’s existing garden at risk.

A future globe prototype should keep the ladybug upright at the centre, rotate the planet beneath it, project touch targets onto its surface and persist latitude/longitude for garden marks. Review drag predictability, horizon visibility, reduced motion and rotation restoration with the founder before adopting it. This is a design judgment, not an educational claim.
