# Lull: Immediate Next Steps

> **Superseded (June 2026).** The founder pivoted to the final-product push: the current
> build is the vision sketch, art direction is locked, and assets ARE being produced —
> see `Docs/NorthStar.md` (governing), `Docs/WindowSlice.md`, `Docs/ArtShoppingList.md`.
> The child-session and parent-conversation methodology below remains the playbook for
> validating each finished slice — run it against the Window slice first.

## The Decision

Freeze the current child experience for the first five observed sessions. Do not add
toys, buy ads, commission a large asset pack, or change the price story before the
sessions are complete.

Why: changing the build between sessions destroys the comparison, while more content
cannot rescue a core loop children do not understand or parents do not value.

## Next 72 Hours

### 1. Make One Real-Device Build

- Install or repair the missing iOS 18.2 platform and simulator runtime in Xcode.
- Run the current build on one supported iPhone and one iPad.
- Verify onboarding, shelf entry, Stack, rotation, background/foreground, parent gate,
  lifetime purchase sandbox flow, restore, sound-off, haptics-off, and Reduce Motion.
- Fix only crashes, blocked flows, unreachable objects, accidental exits, and broken
  purchase behavior before child testing.

Why: a child session is too valuable to waste on an environment or release-flow bug.

### 2. Run Five Child Sessions

- Use the frozen build and the exact method in `Docs/StackHeroValidation.md`.
- Recruit through people already known to the family; do not pay for acquisition or
  collect identifying child data.
- Run at least two sessions on iPhone and two on iPad.
- Include at least one child near age 2, one near age 3, and one near age 4.
- Record observations immediately after each session, not from memory at the end.

Why: five quiet observations will reveal more than another week of adult-led polish.

### 3. Run Five Parent Conversations

- Ask the four unaided value and price questions after play.
- Do not mention the current `$9.99` lifetime price first.
- Record the parent's exact value phrase and price ranges anonymously.
- Ask one final question: "What would stop you from downloading or paying for this?"

Why: child delight and parent willingness to pay are separate proof gates.

## Pass Or Pivot

### Proceed With Stack-Led Launch Polish

Proceed when:

- At least 4 of 5 children independently enter Stack, lift a stone, and repeat a loop.
- At least 4 of 5 recover happily after a topple.
- At least 3 of 5 parents independently name calm play, tactile quality, emotional
  response, or the no-ads/no-pressure promise as valuable.
- No repeated accidental exits, lost objects, or adult explanations are needed.

Then execute:

1. Author Stack-specific clay textures and short foley.
2. Polish Bubbles, Feed, and Bloom to the same sensory standard.
3. Keep Mix-Up and Paint out of launch marketing until they match that standard.
4. Test the lifetime price with parents before final App Store pricing.

Why: the recognizable hero earns attention; four coherent toys earn trust and value.

### Fix The Loop

If children engage but miss one action:

- First reach fails: strengthen Stack's shelf silhouette and opening invitation.
- Lift fails: enlarge selection and make the first stone's lift more obvious.
- Placement fails: increase assisted placement and favor flatter stones.
- Topple dominates: make joyful collapse an explicit primary loop.
- Return handle causes exits: reduce its child-facing salience.

Why: repair the smallest failed step instead of redesigning a loop children already
want.

### Stop Expanding

Stop new toy and marketing work when:

- Fewer than 3 of 5 children repeat any Stack loop without adult help.
- Fewer than 2 of 5 parents can name a reason to pay.
- Parents consistently place the reasonable lifetime price far below the planned price.

Then revisit the product premise, packaging, or business model before spending more.

Why: protecting runway is more important than defending sunk work.

## Safe Parallel Work

These tasks do not invalidate the frozen child test:

- Repair Xcode's iOS platform/runtime installation.
- Add a StoreKit test configuration and automated entitlement tests.
- Verify the parental gate, purchase, and restore flows on a sandbox account.
- Prepare public privacy-policy and support pages.
- Draft App Store screenshots without publishing them.
- Record or source a tiny Stack-only foley test set.

Do not add analytics or third-party SDKs during this phase.

## Spend Guardrail

Until the proof gate passes:

- Paid acquisition: `$0`
- New toy production: `$0`
- Large art or audio commission: `$0`
- Acceptable spend: only a small amount required to run real-device tests or produce
  a narrowly scoped Stack sensory prototype

Why: evidence should unlock spending, not anxiety.
