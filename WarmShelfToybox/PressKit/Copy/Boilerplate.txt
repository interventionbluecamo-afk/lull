SHORT DESCRIPTION
Lull is a quiet digital toybox for children aged 2–6 on iPhone and iPad. Nine tactile-looking activities invite children to wash vehicles, feed friends, make music, sort shapes and explore. Grown-ups choose the sensory settings, the toys on the shelf and a play timer that brings the toybox to rest. There are no ads, social feeds or Lull accounts.

LONG DESCRIPTION
Lull brings nine quiet activities together in a digital toybox for children aged 2–6. Soft felt, warm wood and expressive characters make each toy inviting: scrub and rinse muddy vehicles, offer food to a friend, guide a ladybug, make music, sort dots, match shapes, combine characters, explore a window and pop bubbles.

The design draws on choice, repetition and visible cause and effect. Core play does not require reading, competitive scores or keeping a streak. Grown-ups have a separate area for purchases, sensory settings and choosing which toys appear on the shelf. A play timer lets a grown-up set the allowance for a sitting; a separate wind-down hour warms and dims the rooms in the evening.

Lull uses paid access through an annual plan or a one-time purchase rather than advertising. It has no third-party analytics or Lull accounts, and core gameplay runs locally. Its Montessori inspiration describes a play philosophy, not certification or a promise of learning or health outcomes.
