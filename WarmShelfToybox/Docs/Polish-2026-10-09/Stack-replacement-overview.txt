LULL — WHICH NEW TOY REPLACES STACK
Research overview, October 9, 2026

THE ANSWER
-----------
Build LITTLE WASH, a calm car wash: muddy felt vehicles roll in, the child scrubs,
rinses and buffs them clean, and they drive off sparkling.
It beats all three of my earlier options, Pond included.

Revised ranking:
  1. Little Wash (car wash)           NEW
  2. Little Pond (magnetic fishing)   my old pick, still a strong option
  3. Little Railway (wooden train)    NEW
  Dropped: Little Town, Little Garden, marble run, bakery, sandbox, colour mixing, laundry.


WHAT THE RESEARCH CHANGED
-------------------------
- Garden is out. Sago Mini's gardening game (Jinja's Garden, Apple Arcade, ages 3–6)
  won a 2026 Apple Design Award for Interaction. We'd be compared to an award winner,
  and it overlaps our Meadow.
- Town, marble run and bakery are taken. Pok Pok is the closest rival: calm, Montessori,
  "handcrafted", won an Apple Design Award in 2021, 25 toys. It already has a Town, a
  Marble Machine, Cooking, and Shops (a bakery).
- Pok Pok has no water play, washing, fishing or trains. That's open ground.
- Vehicles are the biggest interest at this age.
  - A Hot Wheels car set was the world's top-selling toy of 2025 (Circana).
  - About 1 in 3 young children has an "extremely intense interest" (DeLoache 2007).
    75% of them are boys, and vehicles and trains are among the most common.
  - Your son lit up at the police officer and firefighter.
- Cleaning is one of the most satisfying things to do in a game.
  - PowerWash Simulator passed 12 million players.
  - "Oddly satisfying" videos all share one thing: you watch something get finished.
  - Washing is classic Montessori practical life (caring for things).
- The gesture is perfect for 2-year-olds. Rubbing needs no precision and no timing.
  - Research on toddlers (Nácher 2015) found double-tap and long-press unreliable.
  - It also found that steering along a path needs precision.
  - Rubbing anywhere avoids all of that.
- The market has a real gap at the top.
  - Car-wash apps for kids are plentiful but ad-funded and low quality.
  - There's no premium, calm one.
  - Sago Mini (Feb 2024) and Toca Boca (Jan 2024) pulled all their one-time-purchase apps.
  - A beautiful, buy-once calm toy now stands out more.


#1  LITTLE WASH — the design
----------------------------
The loop (about 1 minute per vehicle):
  1. A muddy vehicle rolls into a felt-and-wood wash bay, splashing through a puddle.
     Its face is in the windshield and its eyes follow your finger.
  2. Three big tools, any order, rub anywhere:
     - soapy sponge: mud turns to foam
     - hose: rinses foam and mud away
     - towel: buffs it to a shine
  3. When it's clean: a sparkle sweeps across, a happy wiggle, a soft "toot",
     and it drives off. The next one rolls in.

Why it's fun:
  - Instant, with no instructions needed.
  - Visible before and after.
  - The vehicle reacts the whole time: giggles at the brush, sneezes at foam.

Why it looks great:
  - Mud on felt, white foam, water and a sparkle sweep: every frame is a screenshot.
  - "Half-clean fire truck covered in foam" is the App Store hero image.

The cast:
  - fire truck, police car, tractor, school bus, digger, ice-cream van
  - Rare guests: a muddy piglet in a wagon, a hot-air-balloon basket, a toy rocket.

Depth for a 6-year-old:
  - Different dirt: mud, autumn leaves, snow, sand from the beach, paint splashes.
  - Spinning brushes, which feed the "rotation" play children love
    (wheels, washing machines).
  - Rare guests and surprises.

Calm by design:
  - No timer, no score, slow vehicles.
  - Soft water and foam sounds, soft horn, no sirens.

Watch-outs:
  - Its "next visitor" loop resembles Feed. We vary arrivals and keep it short.
  - Foam must not turn into a second Bubbles toy.

Art: about 12 images. Six vehicles drawn clean (mud is added in code), the wash bay,
3 tools and 4 mud textures.
Build: about 3 days. The scrub-to-clean effect is a mud layer erased by touch;
straightforward in SpriteKit.


#2  LITTLE POND — magnetic fishing
----------------------------------
Dangle a magnet bobber, a felt fish swims up and sticks, lift it into the bucket,
tip the bucket and they all splash back.

For:
  - The calmest option.
  - Water is lovely.
  - Magnetic fishing is a perennial bestseller: Pressman's "Let's Go Fishin'" tops
    Amazon's toddler fishing toys (an estimated 9,000 a month).
  - Toddler fishing apps are thin, ad-ridden and barely rated.
  - Nobody premium does it.

Against:
  - Less depth.
  - Little pull for vehicle-loving kids.
  - Lifting at the right moment needs a forgiving auto-catch.

Art: about 10 images. Build: about 2–3 days.


#3  LITTLE RAILWAY — wooden train
---------------------------------
A felt train on a wooden track that's already laid. The child pulls the train to go,
flips the switch points, picks up animal passengers at stations, and sends it through
a tunnel and over a bridge.

For:
  - Trains are a classic deep interest.
  - BRIO-style wooden track plus felt looks iconic.
  - The chugging rhythm is soothing.
  - The only train apps are brand-led (LEGO DUPLO); Toca Train is gone.

Against:
  - Laying track is fiddly by touch, so v1 must use a pre-laid layout.
  - The most work: about 4 days, about 14 images.


WHY THE OTHERS DROPPED
----------------------
- Little Town: Pok Pok has Town; steering along roads needs precision; most work for the payoff.
- Little Garden: an award winner already owns it; overlaps Meadow.
- Marble run: Pok Pok's Marble Machine; building runs is precision-heavy; overlaps Hum.
- Bakery/kitchen: Pok Pok Cooking and Shops; overlaps Feed.
- Sandbox/diggers: lovely, but realistic sand is hard to render well.
- Colour mixing: great Montessori fit, but overlaps Drop Dots colours and Pok Pok Drawing.
- Laundry: toddlers adore watching washing machines, but it's quieter as a first
  impression. Good future toy.
