# Lull — Instagram and Facebook captions

## 1. Play, then rest.

Choose a play limit that fits your family. When time is up, Lull settles into a quiet moon screen. A gentle stopping point, without an alarm.

## 2. Little hands play. Grown-ups set the pace.

Choose the toys on the shelf, turn sound and touch feedback on or off, and select calmer motion. Lull’s family settings sit behind a grown-up check, so children can get on with playing.

## 3. Nine toys. Their own way to play.

Wash a truck, sort a shape, make a little music, or explore a meadow. Nine quiet toys for children aged 2–6 to choose and repeat at their own pace. No ads. No tracking.

## Use notes

- These graphics are creative illustrations, not app screenshots. Do not present illustrated controls as actual app UI.
- The captions work for Instagram or Facebook. Pair each with its matching graphic.
- A grown-up can wake Lull from the timer rest; an hour away also starts a fresh sitting. Avoid “only an adult can unlock it.”
- Avoid “download now” until Lull is publicly available. No pricing or purchase claim is included here.
