# Fresh-eyes awards-style review — build 7

October 10, 2026. Review scope: current artwork, interaction implementation and parent flows. A hands-on session could not be completed because desktop controls could not open the Simulator or respond to Xcode; no new gameplay, listening or device-performance pass is claimed. Founder TestFlight testing remains the decisive check.

**First impression:** a warm, recognizable little world with a clear invitation to explore. The shelf’s current previews now agree with what opens. Needle-felt material is the visual signature; quieter rooms let the toys lead.

**Most compelling:** Feed’s expressive friends; Sleepy Box’s concrete fit-and-return loop; Little Wash’s broad, forgiving action and visible transformation. Live faces, rolling wheels, touch response and dissolving foam can turn authored art into a toy with personality. These are the elements to make excellent before expanding scope.

**The jury concern:** beautiful art is only the beginning. Across nine toys, response and discovery must be as consistent as the material style. Refill variety, shuffled holes and different wash visitors help, but repeat play, tool feel, soft sound and reliable rotation need the founder’s hands-on assessment. Avoid constant decorative movement: characters should react to the child and then settle.

**Parents and conversion:** the welcome explains a calm toybox, family controls, a free week, the three permanently free toys and optional one-time purchase. The parent page now prioritizes controls and concise price/trial/Restore information, with longer explanation expandable. This makes the offer easier to understand; conversion improvement has not been measured.

**Verdict:** a distinctive, increasingly cohesive candidate worth advancing. Award readiness is still a device-experience question. Finish by resolving reproducible friction from this release and completing the store/purchase/accessibility work; another broad redesign would obscure whether the current improvements work.
