Lull 1.0 (8) — founder testing

NEW LAUNCH
The old loading animation is gone. Opening Lull should show the shelf room, then the toys rise into place. Check a fresh launch in portrait and landscape, return from the background, and the transition into the evening tint.

If the old launch screen persists, restart your phone and check again: iOS can cache launch screens. Reinstalling is another option, but deletes local settings and saved creations, so restart first.

ALL NINE TOYS
Gameplay is unchanged from build 7. Please play each toy in both orientations and report the toy, orientation and exact steps for anything broken. Focus on Little Wash cleaning/refill, Feed food and friend variety, Sleepy Box shuffling each completed round, Mix-Up stage fit, Drop Dots colors, Hum spacing and expressions, Meadow visibility, Window and Bubbles.

SOUND AND CONTROLS
Check Sound On in Silent Mode; Sound Off should be silent. Try Haptics Off, Reduce Motion, leaving/returning, headphones, the play timer rest, and VoiceOver on the shelf and two toys. A longer session helps uncover stutters or crashes.

PURCHASE CHECKS (SANDBOX)
If available, test purchase, cancel, pending Ask to Buy, restore, and the free week ending. Do not treat missing store pricing as a successful purchase test.

Please own the hands-on listening/device checks. Automated checks and successful compilation do not establish a device pass. This build is for TestFlight, not public App Review.
