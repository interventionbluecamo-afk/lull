# Build 1.0 (5): founder review

Install and SDK verification status is in the final handoff. This guide describes the intended behavior; it does not claim a device pass.

Please close and reopen Lull after installation. You own the device review; we will upload only after you approve this build.

- **Sound:** entering a toy should be a gentle two-note sound, without the sandy hiss. Listen to Sleepy Box drawer pulls and shape drops. Confirm Sound Off stays silent and Sound On works in Silent Mode.
- **Mix-Up:** check the robot, police officer and firefighter, mixed seams, saved creations and the saved voice. These changes came from Claude before the toy polish.
- **Sleepy Box:** complete two rounds and pull the drawer after each. On the second return, a new box should arrive with rearranged sockets and the same four treasures. Rotate during posting, return and the box exchange.
- **Hum:** slow taps, a fast sweep and a held key should have distinct visual expression. Repress quickly and use two fingers on a key. Let go: glow and singing should settle. Repeat with Reduce Motion, then pause in the grown-up area and resume: keys should gently idle again without reviving held notes.
- **Drop Dots:** four large channels, all five colours available in the visible tray, each dot centred in its channel and resting on the painted floor. Drop, retrieve and pull the tab. Rotate with dots on the board and while holding one.
- **Feed:** in landscape, feed the first visitor and the replacement. Keep the wish bubble visible. Pick up food by its edge and drag that edge to the mouth. Reopen Feed several times to see menu changes. Art additions only appear when their complete asset sets are present.
- **Meadow:** lead the ladybug in circles and towards each boundary. A little flower trail should follow her without hard map edges. Rotate without losing the garden. Night mode and frost reset should stay tidy.
- **Stack:** try sleeping felt stones, varied refills, both landscape stacking spots, the tall-tower bird and a topple. The tray must stay useful and pieces must remain easy to pick up.
- Open all nine toys, return home, background/reopen, and check parent controls and restore purchases. Public release blockers are tracked separately.

Approve with “Build 5 device checks pass; upload it” once it is acceptable. Report failures with the toy and orientation so the next fix is specific.
