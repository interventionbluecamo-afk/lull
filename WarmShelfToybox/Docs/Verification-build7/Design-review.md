# Build 7 — final bounded product and design review

October 10, 2026. Source/art review; founder device review pending.

The distinctive direction is a quiet shelf of physical felt-and-wood toys: children can explore by touch, repeat small actions, and stop without scores or urgency. Little Wash adds broad practical-life gestures and visible cause/effect. Its Montessori connection is an interpretation (care of objects and independent repetition), not evidence of a learning outcome; an early-years educator should review the activity framing.

The strongest work is the warm material world, Feed’s expressive friends, the shape box’s concrete fit feedback, parent-only purchase flow and attention-respecting sound/rest controls. Wash now uses layered moving wheels, live faces, hand-driven lean, tools, clipped mud/foam and water, rather than a static vehicle illustration. Reduce Motion limits the decorative movement.

This pass fixed shelf promises (Wash, four-color Drop Dots, open-air Bubbles, current Mix-Up cast), quieted Feed’s background, added two foods and finite refill variety, simplified parent information, corrected Sleepy Box sockets and repeated-round layout, protected Mix-Up’s stage/live motion from clipping, and deepened Meadow’s night palette.

Jury judgment: a much more coherent candidate for founder testing. Award readiness remains unproven until real touch feedback, sound, readability at small sizes, accessibility, performance and family trust hold up on devices. Do not claim an Apple award, educational efficacy, measured 60 fps, purchase success, or device approval from these source checks. Focus on fixing reproducible defects reported on this build rather than starting another broad redesign.

Before public App Review: founder device/purchase sign-off; current screenshots and metadata; approved IAP review material, agreements, age/privacy answers, support destination and trial presentation. Website source now shows the current nine toys; deployment is not part of this release upload.
