The story

Lull is a quiet toybox for children aged two to six. Nine felt-and-wood toys sit on a warm shelf: a little wash for muddy trucks, a sleepy shape box, a window that turns from day to dusk, and a meadow that wakes as a ladybug passes. Each toy answers touch the way a real one would. There is nothing to read, nothing to win, and nothing to lose. Every sound is made in code, softly, and only in answer to a child's hand. As evening comes the shelf grows warmer and dimmer, and a play timer can end the day with a calm rest. There are no ads, no tracking, and no accounts.
