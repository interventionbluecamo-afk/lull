## Start here

For a wide editorial lead, use [the text-free campaign header](Assets/03-Marketing/2026-10-17/lull-app-store-header.png), 3840 × 1646 PNG. Suggested caption: “Lull promotional illustration with mascot, xylophone, shape sorter and ladybug.” It introduces the brand clearly, but it is an illustration, not gameplay.

Use [the app icon](Assets/01-Brand/lull-app-icon-1024.png), 1024 × 1024 PNG, for app directories and identification. [The brand hero](Assets/01-Brand/lull-brand-hero.png), 1254 × 1254 PNG, provides an alternative square illustration. It is established brand artwork used as a campaign reference; its presence in current onboarding is not asserted.

For an actual game view, start with [the shelf capture](Assets/04-Screenshots/Development-Preview-1.1-build19/lull-dev-preview-1.1-build19-shelf.jpg) or [Little Wash](Assets/04-Screenshots/Development-Preview-1.1-build19/lull-dev-preview-1.1-build19-wash-portrait.jpg). Keep “development preview 1.1 (19), November 4, 2026” in the published caption.

## Screenshots and illustrations

The development previews are actual local Debug 1.1 (19) captures, recorded by the source README as unaltered `simctl` JPEGs from implementation commit `3e1405b`. They were captured on November 4, 2026 using iPhone 18 Pro / iOS 27 and iPad Air 11-inch (M3) / iPadOS 27 simulators. They do not establish physical-device performance, sound, animation timing, accessibility results or App Store availability. They are not current-build-21 screenshots. Meadow captures predate the subsequent beach artwork.

The historical 1.0 (14) images are real-app screenshot composites, not raw captures. Their review-submission provenance does not establish approval or release. Keep their historical label; do not use them to imply the latest app appearance.

The icon, brand hero, cutouts and campaign cards are illustrations. Campaign artwork uses AI-generated imagery based on existing Lull references; available generation prompts are preserved in [Prompt-Records.json](Assets/Provenance/Prompt-Records.json). The cutouts represent shelf objects; the app may add faces, shadows or animation at runtime. These illustrations do not depict physical products for sale. Do not present them as screenshots, real customer photographs or photographs of manufactured toys.

## Reuse and provenance

Use these assets for editorial coverage or promotion of Lull with accurate captions and the stated version/status. Preserve character proportions and existing artwork; do not add fabricated gameplay, awards, testimonials or release claims. For ordinary placement, proportionate sizing is preferable to cutting off the mascot or placing text over the toys. No exclusive-rights, third-party-license or Apple-endorsement claim is made by this kit.

[Asset-Catalog.csv](Asset-Catalog.csv) and [Asset-Catalog.json](Asset-Catalog.json) provide every filename, caption, source path, copy path, image dimensions, status, byte count and SHA-256. `source_path` and `copied_path` are repository-relative; `kit_path` and `prompt_record_file` resolve from this extracted PressKit folder. All 42 hashes were checked against the originals after copying. No image was generated, cropped, resized, retouched, recompressed or composited for this kit.

The prompt records contain exact saved prompt wording and source-document hashes. The app icon has associated component prompts, but its current export is not byte-identical to the separately documented composition file; those records provide partial lineage. The original BrandHero prompt was not located. Screenshot provenance concerns captures rather than a prompt for the whole screen. These limitations are recorded rather than filled in with reconstructed prompts.

Only one existing export of each selected image is included. Duplicate header JPG/native variants and the parallel historical iPad composite set are omitted. Cancelled search-results artwork, rejected concepts, review receipts, payments, private screenshots, child photographs, Stack and Ladybug Kraft materials are excluded.
