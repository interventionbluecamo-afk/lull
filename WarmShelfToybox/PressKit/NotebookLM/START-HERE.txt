QUICKEST ROUTE
1. Make a new notebook and upload 01-Lull-Overview.txt. It is the self-contained factual source.
2. Optionally add 02-Podcast-Script.txt for the proposed host conversation and structure. It is a draft, not real testimony.
3. In Studio, choose Audio Overview. Choose a conversational/two-host format and add the PODCAST prompt from Prompts.txt in the customization field. Generate, listen, and check the result against the overview before publishing.

Google's current help describes text/Markdown uploads and Audio Overview format/customization options. Labels may vary by account or device; current documentation calls the product Gemini Notebook in some places.

For other outputs, use the prompts for a short audio introduction, video outline, carousel, FAQ, interview or slide outline. Prompts are controls for generation, not facts to quote.

IMAGES
Use the PressKit Asset-Catalog.csv or index.html to pick art. The Assets folders distinguish icon/cutouts, actual development screenshots, historical App Store composites and promotional illustrations. Upload only the relevant assets, with their captions/version labels. The ZIP is a delivery container; unzip it before selecting source files.

SOURCE HYGIENE
Do not bulk-upload the whole code repository, old handoffs, private review receipts or mixed old pricing. Those can contradict the current overview. Internal/Production-Notes.md is for maintaining this kit, not a public product source. The separate Ladybug Kraft prototype is excluded from Lull.

BEFORE PUBLISHING
Check names, pronunciations and toy descriptions; confirm any price or availability claim; remove invented endorsements or first-person testing claims. Audio/video generated by the tool may change wording or contain errors. The included script is a guide, not a guarantee of verbatim delivery. For a scripted production with exact wording, use the draft as your own recording script.
