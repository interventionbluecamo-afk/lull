## Feed friends

Use the supplied sprout/grandmother/knithat reference as the material and proportion guide: oversized round head, short body, soft felt fibres, simple warm face, arms down, full body visible, camera straight on, no shadow outside the feet, native transparent background. Create six individually exportable expressions with precisely identical pose, outline, canvas and registration: 1 hungry neutral, 2 satisfied smile, 3 delighted laugh, 4 resting closed eyes, 5 pleasantly surprised, 6 chewing. Keep the mouth centre stable across expressions. No sheet labels.

Generate these friends separately: **Mira**, a child with deep brown skin, short natural coils and a terracotta apron; **Jun**, a child with light olive skin, straight dark hair and a sage cardigan; **Nana**, an older adult with medium brown skin, silver curls and a lavender scarf. Use warm natural skin tones and distinct silhouettes without stereotypes. Filenames use feed-cast-mira-1…6, feed-cast-jun-1…6, feed-cast-nana-1…6. Register/crop all six frames as one set before adding them. Measure the new mouth/head rig rather than assuming an existing friend’s proportions.

## Feed foods

Match the existing felt apple and carrot references: soft warm light from upper left, simple silhouette readable without text, very few large details, no adult face, no eyes or mouth baked in, no drop shadow, one object centred and fully visible, native transparent background. Grapes should be a small cluster of large round berries with one simple sage leaf. The pear should have a plump rounded body with a short brown stem. Export feed-grape and feed-pear imagesets. Keep a clean alpha edge; inspect at 45–70 points.
