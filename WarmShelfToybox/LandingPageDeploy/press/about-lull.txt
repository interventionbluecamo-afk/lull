LULL — PRESS KIT
================

ONE LINE
Calm toys, made for little hands.

BOILERPLATE
Lull is a calm, hand-made digital toybox for children ages 2–4. Every toy is
alive: it wakes the moment a child touches it and settles slowly back to calm —
no scores, no timers, no fail states, no ads. Built on Montessori principles and
the restraint of a good lullaby, Lull even warms and dims toward your family's
bedtime so play winds down on its own. Made for the App Store Kids Category:
private, ad-free, and free of any child-facing prices or pressure.

FAST FACTS
- Name:        Lull
- What:        A calm, living digital toybox for toddlers (ages 2–4)
- Platform:    iPhone & iPad (iOS)
- Category:    Kids / Education
- Price:       Free for 7 days (full toybox), then $9.99 one-time — no subscription
- Free shelf:  Bubbles and Stack are always free, with no child-facing locks
- Privacy:     No ads, no third-party tracking, no data-driven advertising
- Toys:        Bubbles, Stack, Hum, Bloom, Mix-Up, Feed the People, and more

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT
- Living toys: quick to wake, slow to settle — the calm is the point.
- No reward casino: no stars, streaks, or engineered urgency.
- Children never see prices, locks, or upgrade prompts.
- Winds down toward bedtime with the family's own clock.
