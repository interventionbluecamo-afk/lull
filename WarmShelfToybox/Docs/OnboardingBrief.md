## What the reference does right

1. **A vertical timeline of the trial**, icon per step: what happens today, mid-trial,
   the day before it ends, the day it ends. Anxiety dies when time is legible.
2. **Cancel-anxiety answered inline** ("How can I cancel? Super easy…") — not buried.
3. **"No payment required today"** under the CTA — the single most calming line.
4. Social proof, soft friendly art, one screen, one CTA.

## Lull's version (structurally stronger — there is no subscription)

Child-facing onboarding stays near-zero: brand hero breathing → straight into the
Shelf Room. The room is the welcome.

The PARENT trial sheet becomes a warm Lull-themed timeline on linen:

- **Today** — the whole toybox opens. All eight toys, nothing held back.
- **Day 6** — a gentle heads-up (one notification, if allowed; never to the child).
- **Day 7** — the shelf keeps Bubbles + Stack, free forever. Nothing disappears
  mid-play, nothing is ever taken from a child's hands.
- **Whenever you like** — $9.99 once. Yours for always, on every family device.
  No subscription, ever.

Below: "No payment information needed today." + the existing promise rows (no ads,
no tracking, built by one parent, tested nightly by one toddler).

Design language: parent-area themed UI per `NorthStar.md` (clean type, linen, clay
accents — no skeuomorphic toys). New brand hero/icon art (2026-06-10) at top.
