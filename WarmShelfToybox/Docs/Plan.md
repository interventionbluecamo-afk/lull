## Standing protocol

- **Playtests rule.** Founder notes from any device session jump the queue into their
  toy's session. The 2.8-year-old's attention is the spec.
- **Art pipeline**: founder generates from numbered prompts (ChatGPT 5.5, flat #3E6877
  teal, no shadows/vignette; full-bleed only for icons/plates) → a dated folder in
  `Assets/Staging/Drops/` → I key, split, land, verify in-scene, commit. Every slot
  falls back procedural; shipping is never blocked on art.
- **Mixed media is allowed** (founder, June 11): when imagegen fights a slot
  (perspective, seams, regen roulette), build it procedural-but-style-matched with
  imagegen touches (textures, patches, companions) instead of forcing a full regen.
  Perspective law: every asset must match its scene's camera — top-down toys get
  top-down art (the Hum front-view bed under top-down bars is the cautionary tale).
- **Every session ends with**: build green, screenshot verification, commit, and an
  updated checklist here.
