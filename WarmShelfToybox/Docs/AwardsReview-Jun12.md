# Awards-Table Review, Round Two — June 12, night
*Same panel as June 11. Everything since: the Codex art runs landed, the Feed cast
went live, the parent area and onboarding were rebuilt, Meadow was transformed
twice, and a device-QA storm was weathered. The question on the table has changed:
not "is this promising" but "is this submittable."*

---

## What changed our scores since yesterday

**Meadow — from "astonishing for a one-night toy" to the toybox's signature.**
The ladybug (head-forward, teal-free, wing-claps at every capture), claimed land
that closes as ROUNDED shapes (the paper.io read, finally true), five painted
sleepers with real faces that wake by tap, by walk-over, or by the grand loop,
the day ground that looks grown rather than rendered, gardens that survive
rotation, and a toy that *teaches itself* (watch-me + the auto-home close). This
toy went from demo to editorial feature in three days. **Shortlist-ready.**

**Feed — the spell-break is dead.** The authored cast with five expression cells
took the stage; the balloon-head era survives only as a fallback no shipping
device will see. The grandmother behind her counter is a store screenshot. The
sandwich ask reads in one glance. **Shortlist-ready.**

**Drop Dots — the felt rainbow.** Dots match their rings, the pour is honest,
weight is audible, gold is rare and special. The most-improved toy across the
whole project. **Strong.**

**Window — deepest toy in the box, and now explorable.** The persistent lean
with three depths, generous glass that always answers (the dusk dead-band is
fixed), a night light that is a real switch, peek-a-boo curtains, the real-clock
sky. The council's Phase A (watering verb, movable cat, visiting things) would
make it the demo toy of the year. **Shortlist-ready; Phase A is the cherry.**

**Bubbles — honest at last.** One tap one pop, the mother keeps her cascade,
weather not architecture behind it, and magic that grows with a session. The
pearls on morning air is a frame nobody else ships. **Strong.**

**Parent area + onboarding — one system, said out loud.** The membership hero,
the seven-day dots, "the shelf keeps growing" with month chips on BOTH surfaces,
$9.99-once said plainly. This is the most honest paywall the panel has reviewed.
**Strong.**

**Sleepy Box, Stack, Mix-Up, Hum, Shelf** — all holding their June 11 grades or
better (Hum's bed finally makes perspective sense; Mix-Up's 25 citizens align by
measurement; the floor hush lets the box be the show). **Strong across.**

## What still stands between this and the shortlist

1. **AUDIO — the only loud gap left.** Tones are lovely; ambience is absent.
   The scouting file ships tonight (Docs/AudioScouting.md). This is now the
   single highest-leverage work in the project.
2. **Device performance pass** — Release build on the oldest supported iPhone:
   cold starts, 60fps with all art, memory with the grain overlay + plates.
3. **Store composites** — captures exist (the night shelf with the ladybug is
   the opener); captions onto the two plates when copy is blessed.
4. *(Phase A room verbs — not required to submit; required to win.)*

## The verdict

**Feature-complete for submission once audio lands.** Nothing on the gap list is
design; it is finishing and verification. The panel has stopped suggesting and
started waiting.

*— the jury chair, who tapped a sleeping pebble family tonight and watched three
sizes of smile wake up, and would like that on the record* 🏅
