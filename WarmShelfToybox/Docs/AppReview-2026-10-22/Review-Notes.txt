PURPOSE / ACCESS
A calm, Montessori-inspired toybox for ages 2–6 (Kids age band: 5 and under). Nine toys: Bubbles, Drop Dots, Hum, Feed, Mix-Up, Meadow, Window, Sleepy Box and Little Wash. No accounts, login credentials, sample files or special hardware are needed. No chat, public user-generated content or social features. Mix-Up creations stay local.

On first launch, tap Set up Lull. Enter the digits matching the three number words using the gate's keypad (e.g. four · seven · two = 472). Choose family settings and a purchase option, or restore a purchase. Both purchases open all nine toys. Tap a shelf toy to play and its home button to return. The shelf's grown-up button → adult check → Your plan provides purchases, Restore Purchases and subscription management. Purchases and external links are behind the adult check.

GUIDELINE 1.3 / DATA
1. No third-party analytics SDK or service; no gameplay events, child profiles or device identifiers are transmitted to us or an analytics provider.
2. No advertising, ad networks, advertising identifiers or tracking.
3. No children's data is shared with us or third parties. There is no developer backend/cloud database. Apple StoreKit handles purchases, entitlements, restoration and Family Sharing. StoreKit information is used only to determine access/trial/renewal status, not forwarded to another service. We receive no Apple Account credentials or payment-card details. Local app data may be included in the user's own device backups, which we cannot access.
4. Local storage holds family preferences, chosen toys, timer/wind-down state, shown hints, local toy state/Mix-Up creations, gate retry cooldown and cached purchase access. Motion readings are used transiently for shelf tilt, never recorded or transmitted. The optional trial reminder is a permission-based local notification; no remote push. No camera, microphone, contacts, photo or location access. No runtime AI service.

Adult-initiated support/privacy links open the Cloudflare Pages website. Voluntary support email goes through the adult's mail provider to lullios@proton.me (Proton Mail), solely for support. Gameplay data is not uploaded to these services.
