Hello App Review,

Thank you for the detailed feedback. Here are our responses regarding Guideline 1.3 for Lull: Quiet Toybox.

1. Third-party analytics
No. Lull contains no third-party analytics SDK or service. We do not collect or transmit children's gameplay, usage events, identifiers, or profiles to an analytics provider. There are no child or parent app accounts.

2. Third-party advertising
No. Lull contains no advertising, ad network SDK, advertising identifiers, or ad tracking. There is no ad-network policy to provide.

3. Sharing and storage
Lull does not send children's data, gameplay, saved creations, or device identifiers to us or third parties. There is no developer-operated server or cloud database.

Apple StoreKit handles In-App Purchases, entitlement verification, restoration, and Family Sharing. Apple processes the transaction through the user's App Store account. Lull receives StoreKit transaction/entitlement information to determine access and renewal or trial dates; it does not receive payment-card details or the user's Apple Account credentials. We do not forward StoreKit transaction information to another service.

Settings and saved Mix-Up creations are stored in the app's local storage on the device. They may be included in the owner's device backup according to their Apple backup settings; we cannot access those backups.

The grown-up area includes optional external support/privacy links. The website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, and a grown-up who chooses to email support sends their email address and message through their mail provider to our Proton Mail support mailbox, solely for support correspondence. These are adult-initiated support resources, not gameplay or analytics services. Lull does not automatically upload app data to them.

4. Other user or device data
Lull does not collect user or device data for transmission to us. Local data is used only to operate the app: onboarding completion, sound/haptic/motion preferences, chosen shelf toys, play-timer and wind-down settings/state, shown hints, local toy state and Mix-Up creations, parental-gate retry cooldown, and cached purchase-access/trial/renewal information.

Device-motion readings are used transiently on-device for the shelf's tilt effect. They are not recorded or transmitted. An optional trial reminder uses Apple's local notifications on that device, with permission; there is no remote push service. The app does not request access to camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location. No runtime AI service is used. There are no accounts, chat, or publicly shared user-generated content.

Guideline 4
We have replaced the system-keyboard parental gate with a keypad inside the gate itself, keeping the question and answer controls together. We have also constrained the iPad welcome, family settings, plan, and privacy screens to readable widths with scrolling for content that needs more space. These changes are in corrected version 1.0 (16).

Thank you.
