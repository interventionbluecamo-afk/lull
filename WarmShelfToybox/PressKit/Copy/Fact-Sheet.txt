LULL — FACT SHEET
Prepared November 13, 2026. Product description, not a launch announcement.

NAME: Lull: Quiet Toybox
SHORT NAME / PRONUNCIATION: Lull; rhymes with “dull.”
TAGLINE: Calm toys, made for little hands.
CATEGORY: Children's digital toybox / quiet play.
INTENDED AUDIENCE: Children aged 2–6; purchases and settings for grown-ups.
PLATFORMS: iPhone and iPad. Project deployment target: iOS/iPadOS 16 or later.
TOYS: Bubbles, Drop Dots, Hum, Little Wash, Feed, Window, Mix-Up, Sleepy Box, Meadow.
VISUAL LANGUAGE: Simple tactile-looking felt and wood, softened forms, restrained detail, consistent perspective and contact shadows within each toy.
PLAY: Visual choices, touch/drag interaction, repetition, no competitive score or streak system. Montessori-inspired approach; no certification or proven learning-outcome claim.
FAMILY CONTROLS: Play timer, wind-down hour, sound, haptics, calmer motion, shelf toy selection and a grown-up check.
TIMER: Foreground play per sitting; Off/15/30/45/60 minutes. A calm moon-rest screen pauses play. A grown-up can wake Lull; an hour away starts a fresh sitting.
WIND-DOWN: Evening warmth/dimming. Separate from the timer; not a sleep treatment or device lock.
PRIVACY: No ads, third-party analytics or Lull accounts; no developer collection of children's play data. Local storage and Apple StoreKit purchase services have distinct roles. Voluntary support email is used for correspondence.
OFFLINE: Core toys use bundled assets and run locally. App Store product loading, purchases and restoration need connectivity.
ACCESS MODEL: All nine toys require paid access or an eligible subscription trial. Lull Family is annual/auto-renewing; Keep Forever is a non-consumable one-time purchase. Family Sharing is supported in the documented configuration.
PRICE / PUBLIC AVAILABILITY: Not freshly verified for this kit. Use the current Apple purchase flow for terms. Do not publish a price, release date or “available now” based only on this file.
