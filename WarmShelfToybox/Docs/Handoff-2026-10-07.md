## What changed

### Feed — the counter (founder: "table not matching up visually")
Problem: one thin plank floated mid-screen; the friend's round bottom and a cream key patch
poked out under it; the food hung off the plank's front face; a big empty floor sat below.
Now (`FeedScene.swift`, `CharacterNode.swift`):
- The child is the shopkeeper. A deep counter runs from the table line to the bottom of the
  screen, built from the authored plank: its top face cut into board strips with staggered
  seams, and its front edge as the near lip (`buildShopCounter`, `CounterArt`).
- Friends stand behind the far edge, seen chest-up. The counter hides exactly the bottom 30%
  of each painted figure (`standHeight(hidingBottom:)`). Tall friends tuck lower rather than
  clip on short landscape screens (`paintedHeadroom`).
- Every food stands on its own wooden plate (`feed-pedestal`) on the countertop, well below
  the friend's mouth. Fresh food rises from under the counter onto its plate.
- Landscape phones: smaller food and friend (food was a third of the screen tall).
- The first-time hint points at the wanted food.
- Mock: `Assets/DesignPass-02/feed-counter-before-after.png`, `feed-counter-portrait-landscape.png`.

### Drop Dots — dots drop in correctly (founder: "drop in incorrectly")
Problem (measured on the art): the bottom dot sat half a dot below the painted channel floor,
on the rail; the rings sit on a wider pitch than the channels, so outer dots landed on channel
walls; dots drew over the felt rings, the rail and the tray; two handles; a heavy dark pad
under every played dot; a moon carving that read as a letter C.
Now (`DropDotsScene.swift`, board/coin art):
- Dots rest on the painted floor and in the middle of the painted channels (`channelFx`).
- The board is sized to the stack, so a full column of four looks full.
- A dropped dot sinks into its ring, passes behind the wood and appears in its channel; the
  pour slides behind the rail; tray dots stand in the trough behind its front lip.
- One handle: the knob baked into the rail is painted out; the walnut pull sits on the rail
  and pours on release or a short pull, never on touch-down (a resting palm can't empty it).
- Plain clay coins (`dropdots-token-blank`), light resting shadow.
- Mock: `Assets/DesignPass-02/dropdots-seated.png`.

### Sleepy Box — posting
A posted shape is clipped to its socket outline and slips back into the dark behind the rim,
instead of shrinking to a dot in front of the box (`dropPiece`).

### Meadow — visual cohesion (founder: "cool but could use some visual cohesiveness")
- Ground and moss are tiled at their painted resolution (they were stretched ~5× into blur).
- Spring is one world-aligned moss surface revealed where the ladybug walks, with a soft felt
  edge and muted colour, instead of hundreds of re-stretched moss circles with rings.
- Honest coverage drives the warm glow and the full-bloom moment (pacing kept); node count
  no longer grows forever.
- The camera stops at the world edge; the white "highlighter" trail is gone; flat vector
  tufts no longer sit on the felt; side-view landmarks aren't spun; one warm shadow each.
- Mock: `Assets/DesignPass-02/meadow-before-after.png`.

### Art pipeline
- `Tools/Art/despill.py`: turns the teal key-background shadow and fringe into a soft warm
  shadow. Applied to Meadow and Drop Dots art (before/after:
  `Assets/DesignPass-02/despill-before-after.png`). Don't run it blindly on art that is meant
  to be dark blue-green (night trees, skies).
- New art drop folder tracked by Git: `ArtDrops/2026-10-07/`.

### App icon (proposal, not shipped)
Directions at real sizes: `Assets/DesignPass-02/icon-directions.png`,

belly into the sky, built as Icon Composer layers (background, stone) so the light, dark,

  grown-up check (single-digit sums are passable by 4–5-year-olds), timer policy.

## Apple Design Award checklist (what a jury will notice)

- One coherent material world in every toy (the Meadow/Feed/Drop Dots work moves toward this).
- A layered icon with dark and tinted appearances.
- Accessibility: VoiceOver on every toy, Dynamic Type and real switches in the grown-up area,
  Reduce Motion paths that keep cause and effect.
- No dead ends, no silent touches, no clipped art in any orientation or device size.
- The ending ritual: the app gets sleepy rather than cutting play off.
