  • Bubbles Free

    Pop soft bubbles and find the little treasures some carry.

  • Drop Dots Free

    Sort felt dots into their colour homes, find rainbows, and pour them out again.

  • Hum Free

    Touch the singers to make gentle music, alone or in chords.

  • Little Wash

    Scrub muddy trucks clean with a sponge, a hose, and a towel.

  • Feed

    Each friend asks for a snack. Give it, and watch them chew and smile.

  • Window

    Turn the day into evening and see who visits.

  • Sleepy Box

    Post each shape into its hole and open the drawer.

  • Meadow

    Lead a ladybug through a waking meadow and find her five little ones.
